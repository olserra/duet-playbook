# DUET Playbook

> A community guide to getting useful help from Instinct. DUET is the umbrella idea: the human sets direction, the machine amplifies capacity. This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Instinct.

**Start here:** [Install The Configured Assistant](../packs/configured-assistant/INSTALL.md) · [Browse packs](../README.md#-the-packs) · [Read the DUET protocol](https://duetprotocol.surge.sh) · [Português](duet-playbook.pt.md)

## 1. Have a conversation before you automate

Pick one real task, describe the result you need, provide the context that matters, and set limits. Then ask for a sample. Only schedule a routine after the sample is useful.

Adapt this message and send it **in your private chat**, not a public GitHub issue:

```text
Help me plan my day. First ask for my top three priorities and the commitments
I can't miss. Then show me a short sample briefing for tomorrow. Don't send
messages to anyone else or schedule routines until I approve the details.
```

It works because it names an outcome, asks for missing context, and makes the first result reviewable. No special prompt syntax needed.

## 2. The five-step loop

1. **Name the work.** A decision brief, tomorrow's plan, or a deadline check.
2. **Give relevant context.** Time limits, preferences, and sources. Keep private details in your private chat, never in this repository.
3. **Set boundaries.** Say what can be researched or drafted, and what you want to review first, especially messages, purchases, and sensitive decisions.
4. **Review a sample.** Correct the length, tone, format, or sources.
5. **Add a routine only when useful.** Agree on the time, channel, frequency, and alert threshold. Pause it if it becomes noise.

## 3. Build your first rhythm

Start with **one** useful moment. [The Configured Assistant](../packs/configured-assistant/INSTALL.md) offers a morning brief, evening planning, a coaching prompt, a scoreboard, and watchers. Setup asks questions and shows a sample. You can install one module instead of the whole pack and approve only the routines you want.

After setup, try: "show me tomorrow's evening brief now", "make it shorter", "move the morning brief to 6:30", or "pause everything this week". Check what actually changed.

## 4. Alerts without noise

Define **what changed**, **which source to check**, **how often**, and **when to stop**. Ask for a source and timestamp for changing facts. Do not confuse a remembered number with live data. Check account restrictions before frequent checks of logged-in sites.

[Market Watch](../packs/market-watch/INSTALL.md) covers indices, FX, crypto, and chosen alerts. It reports facts, not investment advice. [Inbox Zero Chief](../packs/inbox-zero-chief/) remains a scaffold, not a finished pack.

## 5. Check the work

For current claims, check sources. Before a message goes out, review its text, recipient, and channel. Before spending money or credits, check the final price, cancellation terms, and address. If an answer misses, name what failed and ask for a revision. **Delegate repetitive work without outsourcing your judgment.**

## 6. DUET and the community

The [DUET protocol](https://duetprotocol.surge.sh) offers twelve principles for human-AI partnership. The playbook turns those ideas into small tasks to try today; packs are reusable examples. The DUET ebook is on its way. No release date or link yet; the book is not required to use the playbook.

Contribute through [the guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md): add a generic example, improve a translation, or suggest a pack. Never post passwords, tokens, private messages, family names, or third-party data. If a product update would solve a problem outright, don't promise a permanent pack as its workaround.
